The Skyborne

The Shen’dorai: Sovereigns of the Endless Blue

New Race: WoW Forever

Origin of the Air Elves

The Skyborne, or Shen’dorai, are High Elves who diverged from their kin during the height of the arcane corruption. By forging an ancient pact with the elemental lords of Air, they escaped the terrestrial shackles of Azeroth, ascending to the floating spires of Zephras Isle.

Culture & Abilities

Wind-Walking

The Skyborne possess the innate ability to glide through the air, allowing them unmatched mobility in vertical environments.

Aero-Arcana

Their magic is a fusion of traditional Arcane study and elemental air manipulation, resulting in devastating storm-based spells.

The Zephras Pact

A sacred oath that grants them protection from the elements but binds them to the laws of the High Skies.
