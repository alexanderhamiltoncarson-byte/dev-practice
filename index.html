WoW: Forever

MAJOR RELEASE: NOV 4, 2026

A Reimagined Azeroth

World of Warcraft: Forever is a permanent, reimagined game mode. It exists in a unique "time bubble" set after the events of Warcraft III: Forsaken Kingdom but before the Molten Core, diverging from the retail timeline to explore untold stories.

🕊️
The Skyborne (Shen’dorai)

New playable race. High Elves who forged a pact with the Air elementals to survive the corruption of their kin.

⚔️
New Class Combinations

Breaking tradition with the introduction of Forsaken Paladins, Night Elf Paladins, and Dwarf Shamans.

📍
Expanded Geography

Visit entirely new lands like Zephras Isle and the Riverglades through 1,000+ new quests.

📉
The Eternal Level 60

A commitment to eliminate "FOMO"—the level cap stays at 60 indefinitely while content continues to evolve.

Status: Coming November 4, 2026

The Cosmic Order

The Light

The force of holy energy, creation, and hope. It is the primary power of the Naaru and the Paladins of the Alliance.

The Void

The opposite of the Light. A chaotic force of shadows and whispers that seeks to consume all existence.

The Arcane

The energy of Order. It is the fuel for magic, allowing mages to reshape reality through logic and study.

The Fel

The energy of Disorder. Destructive and corrupting, Fel is the primary tool of the Burning Legion.
